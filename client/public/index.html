How It Works & Access Roles

Focused Workspaces for Students, Faculty & Administrators

Each role accesses only the booking, maintenance, or governance tools it needs.

Students

Academic study & project rooms
  • Browse CICS rooms across Floors 1–5 and check open hours before signing in.
  • Request Learning classrooms (101–506) with optional BSIT block tags (e.g., IT-3106).
  • Report classroom or restroom maintenance issues directly to facilities staff.

Professors

Computer labs & instructional rooms
  • Reserve Computer Labs 1–6, SMART Classroom, and Physics/Tech/IT laboratories.
  • Verify open slots ahead of class sessions and cancel unused bookings cleanly.
  • Escalate projector, workstation, or HVAC issues with severity tracking.

Administrators

Approvals, repairs & analytics
  • Approve or reject pending bookings with audit-logged administrative notes.
  • Triage maintenance tickets, switch room statuses, and manage user roles.
  • Review admin-only CICS utilization charts derived strictly from recorded bookings.
